Governance Workflow (Finance Pilot)

1. Submit Action

Send invoice approval, payment, or workflow to the runtime endpoint. Include agent ID, action context, and policy version.

2. Policy Evaluation

Deterministic gate resolves entitlement, risk, threshold, and approval context in ~11ms.

3. Decision & Proof

Returns PASS, BLOCK, REVIEW, or UNSUPPORTED. Includes proof hash, constraint results, and audit trail.

4. Audit Trail

Record committed to tamper-evident hash chain. Replay-safe nonce ensures idempotency and prevents duplicate processing.
