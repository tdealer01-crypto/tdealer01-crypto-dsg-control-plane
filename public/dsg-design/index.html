Core Capabilities

⛔ Gate AI Before Execution

Submit high-risk actions to a deterministic policy engine. Receive PASS, BLOCK, REVIEW, or UNSUPPORTED decisions in milliseconds, before any model runs.

🔗 Tamper-Evident Records

Every decision recorded as SHA-256 hash chain with policy version, input hash, proof hash, and constraint outcomes. Replay-safe with cryptographic nonce and idempotency keys.

📋 Compliance Evidence Pack

EU AI Act Annex IV mapping, CCVS L1–L5 evidence, 3,389 automated tests, and 72.08% mutation score. Export as JSON or SARIF for audit review.

⚡ No LLM Latency

Policy evaluated in static deterministic gate. No round-trip overhead. 10–100× faster than frameworks that route decisions through language models.
