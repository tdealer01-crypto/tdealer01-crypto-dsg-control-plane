### 12:00 PM UTC: Segment Responses by Quality

For each response, score 1-10:

- **9-10:** "Can we do a demo next week?" — Schedule immediately
- **7-8:** "Tell me more" — Send use-case guide + calendar link
- **5-6:** "Interesting but not now" — Add to nurture email list
- **1-4:** "Not interested" — Log as disqualified

**Track in CSV:**
- Add "Response_Received_Date"
- Add "Response_Text" (brief)
- Update "Lead_Score" based on response quality
- Add "Next_Action" (Schedule demo / Send guide / Nurture / Disqualify)

### 2:00 PM UTC: Engage with Responders

For each response, reply within 24 hours:

- **Score 9-10:** "Great! Let's get you on the calendar. When's good for a 15-min demo? [calendar link]"
- **Score 7-8:** "Thanks for the interest! Here's a use-case guide for [their industry]. Let's demo when you're ready. [calendar link]"
- **Score 5-6:** "Happy to chat later! For now, here's our free tier if you want to test. [free tier link] I'll follow up in 2 weeks."
- **Score 1-4:** No reply needed; log as disqualified

- Action: Send missing cold emails Friday afternoon + Mon morning

**Response rate < 3%:**
- ⚠️ Suboptimal but not blocking
- Action: Pivot messaging to "Prevent-Chargebacks" angle (historically 25% higher response)
- Action: Expand email list +20 more prospects
- Action: Post to 5 more crypto communities (DeFi Llama Discord, Crypto Devs Slack, etc.)

**Demo calls booked < 5:**
- ⚠️ Contingency trigger
- Action: Direct outreach to top 10 warm leads (score 8-9) with urgent message: "Audit season is starting. Let's do a 15-min demo this week to see if DSG ONE is a fit."

- Action: Include free tier CTA in all follow-up emails
