## Evidence classes / truth boundary

- **VERIFIED-GIT**: supported by public GitHub timestamps/history.
- **HASHED-ARCHIVE**: artifact identity is bound by a recomputed SHA-256. Internal archive timestamps alone are not independent proof of public availability on that date.
- **DOI-PUBLIC**: supported by a public repository/DOI record.
- **CLAIMED-IN-ARTIFACT**: wording appears in a historical artifact but is not independently validated by this evidence index.

This record does not claim “world first,” certification, endorsement, legal priority, or independent technical validation. Commercial, account-status, cost, performance, and valuation statements contained in historical material remain claims of those artifacts unless independently corroborated.
