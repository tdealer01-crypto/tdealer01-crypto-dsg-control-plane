## Send Sequencing

### Option A: Cold Email (If you have email list)

```
Day 0 — Email 1 (main): Send to 15-20 people
Day 2 — Email 2 (follow-up): Send to non-responders
Day 5 — Email 3 (objection): Send to non-responders
Day 8 — Email 4 (pricing): Send to people who showed interest but didn't book
Day 12 — Email 5 (win-back): Send to people who downloaded but didn't book
```

### Option B: LinkedIn DMs (If using LinkedIn)

```
Day 0 — Send Email 1 as DM (personalized)
Day 1 — Send 90-sec demo video link as follow-up
Day 3 — Send Email 3 (objection handling) as DM
Day 5 — Personalized call-to-action based on response
```

### Option C: Mixed (Recommended)

```
Days 0-2: LinkedIn DMs (more personal, easier to follow-up)
Days 2-5: Email (more formal, trackable opens/clicks)
Days 5-8: Email follow-ups (if no response)
Days 8+: Skip (move to next prospect)
```

## Anti-Spam Guidelines

✅ **DO:**
- Personalize subject + first line
- Include why you're reaching out (research-backed)
- Respect unsubscribe requests
- Space out follow-ups (2+ days)
- Keep emails under 250 words

❌ **DON'T:**
- Mass-blast identical emails
- Use misleading subject lines
- Over-personalize (too specific = looks like spam)
- Send >3 follow-ups
- Use all caps or excessive punctuation!!!
