# Discovery Call Script — 4-Question Framework

**Duration:** 15-30 minutes  
**Goal:** Understand their fund movement process + qualify for pilot  
**Owner:** PMM (conducting discovery calls)

---

## Pre-Call Prep (5 min before)

### Research
- [ ] Read their website (focus: team size, funding, compliance mentions)
- [ ] Check LinkedIn (find CFO/Compliance contact details)
- [ ] Note: Any recent news about regulatory changes in their segment
- [ ] Prepare: 1 concrete use case for their company

### Setup
- [ ] Calendar invitation sent with Zoom link
- [ ] Add to CRM with "Discovery Call" tag
- [ ] Open their LinkedIn + website during call (reference window)
- [ ] Have demo video link ready (if they ask)
- [ ] Have pricing sheet ready (if they ask)

---

## Call Opening (2 min)

**Script:**

```
Hi [FirstName], thanks for jumping on. I really appreciate your time.

Quick agenda for 15 minutes:
1. I'll understand how you handle fund approvals today
2. I'll share how DSG ONE could help
3. We'll figure out if trying a 2-week pilot makes sense

Does that work?

[Wait for yes]

Great. I'll start: What does fund movement approval look like at [Company] today?
```

**Tone:** Friendly, concise, curious (not salesy)

---

## Question 1: Current Approval Process (4-5 min)

**Goal:** Understand their workflow, pain points, and buyer signals

**Open-ended question:**
```
"Walk me through how a [specific transaction type] is approved at {{companyName}}.

From the moment someone says 'let's do this' to when money actually leaves your wallet — 
what's the process?"
```

**Listen for:**
- Who approves? (1 person, 2 people, committee?)
- How long does it take? (minutes, hours, days?)
- What records are kept? (email, spreadsheet, database?)
- Any blockers or delays?
- Any near-misses or mistakes?

**Follow-ups (if they're being vague):**
- "What does that look like operationally — like, who gets an email?"
- "And if they say no, what happens then?"
- "Do you have a way to audit that decision later?"
- "What happens if the auditor asks to see proof?"

**Score indicator:**
- 🔥 HOT: "We email a spreadsheet to 3 people and hope someone approves it"
- 🌤️ WARM: "We have a workflow, but it's manual"
- ❄️ COLD: "We have an API, fully automated"

---

## Question 2: Audit Trail Pressure (3-4 min)

**Goal:** Understand compliance pain + buying urgency

**Direct question:**
```
"When your auditor (or compliance team) asks: 
'Prove that this $[amount] [transaction] was approved by the right person' — 
what's your answer?"
```

**Listen for:**
- Do they have proof readily available? (Yes/No)
- How long does it take to gather? (minutes/hours/days)
- Can they export it? (PDF/JSON/spreadsheet)
- Do they worry about auditor questions?
- Any compliance gaps they know about?

**Follow-ups:**
- "How often do audits happen?"
- "What framework are they checking against? (SOC2, ISO 42001, EU AI Act?)"
- "What would happen if you COULDN'T prove a decision?"
- "Have auditors ever questioned your approval process?"

**Score indicator:**
- 🔥 HOT: "We scramble for 10+ hours per audit. It's painful."
- 🌤️ WARM: "We have proof, but gathering it is tedious"
- ❄️ COLD: "We're fully compliant and auditors never ask"

---

## Question 3: Financial Motivation (2-3 min)

**Goal:** Understand ROI + budget authority

**Direct question:**
```
"Let's say DSG ONE could cut your audit prep time 80% 
(from 10 hours down to 1.5 hours per cycle). 

What would that be worth to [Company]?"
```

**Listen for:**
- Do they immediately see value? (Yes/hesitant)
- Do they quantify savings? ("That's 40 hours/year, worth $X")
- Do they mention budget? ("We'd need to get approval from...")
- Do they ask about pricing?

**If they're vague, ask directly:**
```
"Assuming we save you 8-10 hours per audit, 
and you do 2-3 audits per year, 
what's that worth in budget terms?"
```

**Score indicator:**
- 🔥 HOT: "That would save us $50K+ in labor. Easy decision."
- 🌤️ WARM: "Yeah, we'd be interested. Depends on price."
- ❄️ COLD: "Nice, but not a priority right now."

---

## Question 4: Product Fit (2-3 min)

**Goal:** Confirm they use Stripe/Solana/OpenAI + understand integration effort

**Direct question:**
```
"Does your fund approval system integrate with [Stripe/Solana/OpenAI], 
or do you have a custom system?"
```

**Listen for:**
- Do they use standard tools? (Stripe, Solana, etc.)
- Do they have an API? (can DSG integrate easily?)
- What's their tech stack?
- Who's their engineer contact (if needed)?

**Follow-ups:**
- "If we built a 30-second integration, would you try it?"
- "Who on your team would need to approve the integration?"
- "How long would it take you to test something new?"

**Score indicator:**
- 🔥 HOT: "Yes, we use Stripe. Our eng team can integrate in 1 day."
- 🌤️ WARM: "We have an API. Maybe 3-5 days of eng time."
- ❄️ COLD: "We have a custom system. Integration would be complex."

---

## Pilot Proposal (if score 7+) (2-3 min)

**If they're interested, propose:**

```
"Based on what you shared, DSG ONE seems like a fit. 

Here's what I'd suggest:

2-week pilot (free):
- We set up a shadow mode on your main fund flow
- You keep doing approvals your normal way
- DSG watches and logs every decision
- After 2 weeks, you see: here's what we would have blocked/approved
- Then you decide: want to move to live mode?

What would you need from us to get started?"
```

**Answer their questions:**
- "Will it slow things down?" — No, shadow mode is read-only
- "How long to set up?" — 30 min onboarding call + 1 hour eng work
- "What if we don't like it?" — Cancel anytime, no commitment
- "Can we integrate with [specific tool]?" — Yes, or we'll do it

**Next steps:**
- Send calendar link for 30-min onboarding call (next week)
- Send integration guide (if tech questions)
- Send pilot agreement (no legal, just 1-pager)

## Scoring System

### After the call, score 1-10:

**Scoring rubric:**

| Question | HOT (3 pts) | WARM (2 pts) | COLD (1 pt) |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Q1: Approval process | Manual, error-prone | Semi-automated | Fully automated |
| Q2: Audit pain | "10+ hours scramble" | "Takes a few hours" | "Fully documented" |
| Q3: Financial value | "$50K+ ROI" | "$5-20K potential" | "Not a priority" |
| Q4: Product fit | "Easy integration" | "Possible, needs eng" | "Custom system" |

**Total: 4-12 points**
- **10-12 (HOT):** Propose pilot immediately ✅
- **7-9 (WARM):** Send info, follow up in 1 week
- **4-6 (COLD):** Polite pass, add to future list

## Handling Objections

### "We already have compliance covered"
**Response:**
```
"That's great! Sounds like you're ahead of the curve.

Quick question: Do you spend significant time gathering audit proof, 
or is it pretty automated today?

[Listen]

If it's manual at all, even a little, DSG could save time. 
But if you're already fully automated, we might not add much value.

Either way, worth 2 minutes to see if it's a fit?"
```

### "We don't have budget"
**Response:**
```
"I totally get it. Budget cycles are tight.

Here's a different angle: If a 2-week free trial showed us 
saving you $5K+ in audit time, would that change the conversation?

[Listen]

That's what we're proposing: free trial first, then decide based on real data."
```

### "We need to talk to [boss/team]"
**Response:**
```
"Absolutely. Who specifically should I follow up with?

[Get name + email]

I'll send them a summary of our chat, and we can coordinate a quick 15-min 
call with the team to answer any questions.

What's the best time for your team next week?"
```

### "Your pricing is too high"
**Response:**
```
"Fair point. Where were you expecting to be?

[Listen]

Here's another angle: 
- We save you 8-10 hours per audit cycle
- That's $2-3K in labor costs per audit
- If we save just 1 audit's worth of time in a year, we've paid for ourselves

Does that math make sense for [Company]?"
```

### "We need more features"
**Response:**
```
"Great feedback. What specific features are you thinking?

[Listen]

Got it. Here's what I'd suggest: 
Start with the core (approval gating + audit trail).
If that works, let's talk about [specific feature] in phase 2.

Does that make sense to try first?"
```

## Call Ending (1 min)

**If they're interested (score 7+):**
```
"Perfect. Here's what I'll do:

1. Send you a calendar link for 30-min onboarding call (just to set up)
2. Send pilot agreement (super simple, 1 pager)
3. Introduce you to our eng team (they'll handle the technical setup)

You'll have audit-ready fund movement governance running in your shadow mode within 1 week.

Sound good?"

[Confirm and close]
```

**If they're not sure (score 4-6):**
```
"Thanks for the honest conversation. Here's what I'll do:

1. Send you a 1-pager with product overview
2. Share a case study from [similar company]
3. Follow up in 1 week to see if anything changed

Does that work?"

[Close professionally]
```

**If they're not interested (score <4):**
```
"I really appreciate your time. Sounds like right now might not be the fit.

If that changes — audits get tougher, or you move fund management — 
feel free to reach out.

Take care!"

[Exit gracefully]
```

## Example Call Transcript (Template)

```
Caller: "Hi Alice, thanks for jumping on. Quick agenda for 15 minutes..."

Alice: "Sure, sounds good."

Caller: "Great. Let me start: Walk me through how a staking payout is approved at Marinade."

Alice: "Sure. Someone in operations writes up the payout plan, 
sends it to the CFO and general counsel for approval. 
They email back yes/no. Takes maybe 24 hours."

Caller: "Got it. And if the auditor asks 'prove that payout was approved' — what do you show them?"

Alice: "We'd pull the email chain and the transaction record from Solana."

Caller: "Makes sense. How long does that take to gather?"

Alice: "Ugh, like 2-3 hours, digging through email, finding approvals..."

Caller: "And you do audits how often?"

Alice: "Twice a year, mandatory compliance."

Caller: "So you're spending ~5-6 hours per year just gathering proof. 
What would it be worth if we could cut that to 20 minutes per audit?"

Alice: "That's... honestly could save us $2-3K in labor costs. Pretty significant."

Caller: "Got it. And does your payout system talk to Solana directly, or is it manual?"

Alice: "We have an API integration with Solana."

Caller: "Perfect. So here's what I'm thinking: 
Try a 2-week pilot where DSG watches your payouts, 
logs everything with proof. 
After 2 weeks, you see all the decisions we made. 
Then you decide if you want to go live.

Free, no commitment. What do you think?"

Alice: "That sounds... actually pretty reasonable. How would we set that up?"

Caller: "Simple: 30-min onboarding call with our team, 
they handle the Solana integration (1 hour), 
and you're live in shadow mode next week. 
I'll send a calendar link in my follow-up email."

Alice: "Okay, let's do it."

Caller: "Perfect. I'll get everything to you today. 
Talk soon?"

Alice: "Sounds good. Thanks!"
```

## Tips for Success

✅ **DO:**
- Listen more than you talk (60/40 rule)
- Take notes visibly (they see you care)
- Ask follow-ups based on their answer (not robotic)
- Acknowledge their concern (even if objection)
- Be honest if you don't know ("I'll find out and get back to you")

❌ **DON'T:**
- Pitch too hard (discovery is about listening)
- Talk about features they didn't ask for
- Argue about objections (agree, then redirect)
- Rush (let pauses happen)
- Promise things eng can't deliver
